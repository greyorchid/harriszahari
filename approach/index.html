Method selection

Methodology is a decision, not an identity.

I use Lean for flow and waste, Six Sigma for variation and root causes, BPM for end-to-end process governance, Agile for iterative delivery, Design Thinking for user-centered ambiguity, and PMO controls for portfolio execution.

The objective is a governed result. The method serves that objective.
