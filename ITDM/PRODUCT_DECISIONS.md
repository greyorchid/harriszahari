# Product Decisions

## What is automated

- Two independent assessor runs.
- Round-level evidence isolation.
- Structured output validation.
- Evidence-ID allowlisting.
- ECI calculation and contradiction caps.
- Inter-assessor comparison.
- Feedback storage and export.

## What remains human

- Evidence-package approval.
- Contamination and protocol-breach review.
- Interpretation of disagreements.
- Causal claims.
- Recommendation approval.
- Client-facing language.
- Rubric and threshold changes.

## Immediate feedback questions

1. Did users understand Base ECI versus Effective ECI?
2. Which anchors caused hesitation or disagreement?
3. Did evidence citations support the finding?
4. Did the diagnosis change a decision?
5. Which outputs felt overly confident or insufficiently specific?
6. Was the human review burden acceptable?
